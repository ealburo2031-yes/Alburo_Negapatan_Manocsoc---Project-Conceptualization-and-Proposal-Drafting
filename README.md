# Student Study Tracker
The Student Study Tracker is a web-based application designed to help students organize their school 
assignments, monitor deadlines, and track their academic progress. It allows users to record their 
tasks, update their status, and view summaries of their completed and pending activities. The 
application will use Python for its programming functions and JSON files to store and manage 
information.

# Problem Statement

Students often struggle to organize their school assignments, remember deadlines, and monitor their 
academic progress. Without a proper tracking system, they may overlook important tasks or have 
difficulty identifying which activities need more attention. This project aims to provide a simple and 
organized platform where students can manage their academic responsibilities and monitor their progress 
efficiently.
